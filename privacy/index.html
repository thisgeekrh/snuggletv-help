Privacy Policy

Short version: SnuggleTV doesn't collect anything about you or your kids. There are no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Effective October 19, 2026

What SnuggleTV collects

Nothing. SnuggleTV has no servers of its own, and the developer never receives your name, email, viewing history, location or any other information from the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from anyone.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps these things on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Your Plex sign-in token, stored securely in the device's Keychain.
  • Kid profiles (the name or nickname you type, an animal and a color), their rules, favorites and settings.
  • Where each video was stopped and which videos were finished, so "Continue watching" and auto-downloads work.
  • Saved videos and posters you choose to download.

Deleting the app removes all of this from the device.

Plex

SnuggleTV connects directly to plex.tv (to sign in and find your servers) and to your own Plex servers (to list and play videos). It sends Plex the information Plex needs to do that, such as a random identifier for this copy of the app, the app's name, and playback progress so "continue watching" works in other Plex apps too. That information goes to Plex and your servers, not to the developer, and is covered by Plex's privacy policy.

iCloud sync

If "Sync with iCloud" is on, kid profiles, settings, watched spots and finished videos are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they match on your other devices. The developer can't see this data. Your Plex sign-in is never synced. You can turn sync off in Grown-ups at any time.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer doesn't receive your payment details.

Local network

The app asks for permission to find devices on your local network so it can talk to a Plex server in your home. It only uses this to reach your Plex servers.

Children

SnuggleTV is designed for families with young children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, shows no ads, and has no links to other apps or websites in the kids' screens. Settings, purchases and links are behind a grown-up lock.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
