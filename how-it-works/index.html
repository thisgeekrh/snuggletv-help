How SnuggleTV works

SnuggleTV plays videos from your own Plex Media Server. It doesn't come with shows of its own, so you'll need a Plex server with some kids' videos on it (yours, or one a family member shares with you).

Getting started

  1. Link your Plex account. Open SnuggleTV and it shows a short code. On any phone or computer, go to plex.tv/link, sign in to Plex and type the code. Your password never goes into SnuggleTV.
  2. Pick your servers. Choose which Plex servers the kids can watch from. If the same show is on two servers it only shows up once, and plays from whichever server is reachable.
  3. Make a profile for your child. Pick their name, an animal and a color, then choose which libraries (or exactly which shows and movies) they can see.
  4. Set the rules. Choose a daily limit in minutes or in shows, and a grown-up PIN. That's it. Hand it over!

Shows a dayLittle ones understand "3 more shows" better than minutes. The current show always finishes first.

iCloud syncKids, rules and watched spots match on every iPhone and iPad.
EspañolThe kids' screens are also in Spanish.

Offline and auto-downloads Unlock

With the one-time unlock you can save videos to the device in Low (about 480p, smallest), Medium (720p) or Original quality. For Low and Medium, your Plex server converts the video first (you'll see "Plex is getting it ready"), then it downloads.

Auto-downloads keeps the next few episodes of each child's favorite shows saved, and removes episodes once they've been watched to the end. It runs when the app opens and overnight while the device is charging and on Wi-Fi.

Free and unlocked

Unlock SnuggleTV (one-time purchase): offline downloads, auto-downloads and more than one kid. No subscription and no ads. It works on all your devices with the same Apple Account and can be shared with Family Sharing. Use "Restore purchase" in Grown-ups on a new device.

Tip: keep little ones inside the app

iPhone and iPad have a built-in feature called Guided Access. Turn it on in Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, open SnuggleTV, then triple-click the side (or Home) button to lock the device to SnuggleTV until you enter your passcode.
