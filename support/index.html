Common questions

Do I need a Plex server?

Yes. SnuggleTV plays the videos on your own Plex Media Server (or one that's shared with you). It doesn't include any shows itself. Plex Media Server is free from plex.tv.

It can't find my server, or says it can't reach it.

Make sure the Plex server is on and the iPhone or iPad is on the same Wi-Fi. If iOS asked to "find devices on your local network", choose Allow (or turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → SnuggleTV). Then open Grown-ups and tap Reconnect. Watching away from home needs Remote Access turned on in your Plex server's settings.

A video says "This video won't play right now."

Your Plex server may need to convert that file for iPhone and iPad. Check that the server is running and isn't too busy. Saving the video in Low or Medium quality is a good fallback.

A download is stuck on "Plex is getting it ready".

Your Plex server converts Low and Medium downloads before sending them, which can take a while on smaller servers. Downloads continue in the background. If one fails, open Grown-ups → Manage downloads and tap Retry. Downloads also pick up again by themselves after the app is closed.

Downloads say "Not enough free space".

SnuggleTV leaves room for your device, so downloads wait when it's almost full. Delete a few saved videos in Grown-ups → Manage downloads, or free up space in Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage.

Auto-downloads isn't saving anything.

Auto-downloads needs the unlock, the switch turned on in Grown-ups, and at least one favorite (Grown-ups → Kids → a kid → Favorites, or the ⭐ on a show). It waits for Wi-Fi unless "Download over cellular" is on.

I forgot my grown-up PIN.

On the PIN screen, tap "Forgot PIN? Answer a math question". You can then change or remove the PIN in Grown-ups.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Grown-ups, tap "Unlock or restore a purchase", then Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account (or are in the same Family Sharing group).

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Some shows are missing for one of my kids.

Check that kid's settings in Grown-ups: which libraries they can see, any hand-picked titles, and their age rating. Shows without a rating are hidden if "Show titles with no rating" is off.
